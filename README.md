# PressGo 🚀  
### Smart Aggregator-Based Laundry Management & Delivery Platform

PressGo is a modern full-stack laundry aggregation platform inspired by systems like Uber and DoorDash, designed to connect customers, laundry shops, and delivery drivers through a centralized digital ecosystem.

The platform focuses on automating laundry order management, pickup & delivery coordination, payment handling, commission tracking, and real-time workflow management through a scalable backend architecture.

---

# ✨ Core Vision

PressGo aims to digitize and modernize the traditional laundry service industry by providing:

- On-demand laundry booking
- Pickup & delivery management
- Multi-role workflow handling
- Smart order tracking
- Automated notifications
- Revenue & commission management
- Scalable API-first backend architecture
